About me

I'm a 25-year-old creative based in Singapore. After two years in corporate real estate, I decided to stop shelving my ideas and start building them, so I left my job to develop apps full-time.

Today I take ideas from a notebook sketch to a working product. I write the code, shape the product and figure out how to get it in front of the right people. My first app launches in January 2027.

This site is where I document that journey, including what works, what doesn't and what I learn along the way. If you've been sitting on an idea of your own, I hope it gives you the nudge to start.
